# DEV-2026-014 Post-use filter integrity test failure, batch ALD-26-109

## 1. Summary
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Discovered | 2026-03-05 |
| Product / batch | ALD-101 / ALD-26-109 |
| Equipment | Fill line FL-2, SG-22 filter, integrity tester FIT-04 |
| Classification (QA) | Major |
| Investigator | Alex Morgan, QA Analyst |
| State | Closed 2026-03-30 |

## 2. Immediate actions
Filling stopped after the post-use test. The batch was placed on QA hold.

## 3. Description of event
The post-use bubble point test of the SG-22 sterilising filter gave **3.1 bar**, below the
acceptance criterion of ≥ 3.4 bar (SOP-QC-041 §4). After re-wetting with 1.0 L of water for
injection, the retest gave **3.6 bar** (pass) per SOP-QC-041 §6.3.

## 4. Investigation and root cause
- The FIT-04 printout and calibration were checked: no fault found.
- Batch record review showed the operator flushed **0.5 L** of water for injection before the
  first test instead of the required 1.0 L. The master batch record step read "flush filter"
  without stating the volume.
- **Root cause:** incomplete wetting of the filter before the first test, caused by an
  ambiguous master batch record instruction. The passing retest after full wetting confirms
  the filter was integral.

## 5. Impact assessment
The filter was integral throughout filtration; sterility assurance of ALD-26-109 is not affected.

## 6. CAPA
CAPA-2026-006: state the wetting volume in the master batch record and retrain FL-2 operators.

## 7. Closure
QA approved the investigation on 2026-03-30. Aldermoor was notified within the quality agreement timelines.

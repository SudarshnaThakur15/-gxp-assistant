## 2. Immediate actions
On arrival at the Aldermoor warehouse the shipment was quarantined at 2–8 °C and the logger
file was downloaded (SOP-PRD-052 §5).

## 3. Description of event
The data logger recorded a maximum of **11.4 °C** for a cumulative **3 hours 20 minutes**
during a delayed hand-over at the airport. All other readings were within 2–8 °C.

## 4. Investigation and root cause
The courier confirmed a 4-hour delay caused by a missed connecting flight. The shipper was
used within its qualified duration. **Root cause:** transport delay outside NorthBridge control.

## 5. Impact assessment
The excursion (≤ 25 °C, well under 24 hours cumulative) is within the time out of
refrigeration supported by stability data (SPEC-ALD-001 §5). No impact on product quality.

# QA-ALD-001 Quality Agreement: NorthBridge CDMO and Aldermoor Biologics (v1.0)

## 1. Parties
**Contract giver:** Aldermoor Biologics. **Contract acceptor:** NorthBridge CDMO, site NB-1.

## 2. Products and activities
Aseptic formulation, sterile filtration and filling of **ALD-101** (monoclonal antibody,
100 mg/mL solution in 10 mL glass vials) on fill line **FL-2**.

## 3. Confidentiality
Aldermoor documents and data are accessible only to NorthBridge staff assigned to the
Aldermoor programme.

## 4. Documentation
NorthBridge maintains the master batch record for ALD-101. Executed batch records are
retained as defined in SOP-QA-030 or for ten years, whichever is longer.

## 5. Deviations
NorthBridge manages deviations under SOP-QA-012 and shares the investigation report with
Aldermoor on request.

## 6. Notification timelines
NorthBridge notifies the Aldermoor QA Director of:
- **critical deviations within 1 business day** of discovery;
- major deviations within 3 business days;
- minor deviations in the monthly quality report.

## 7. Batch certification
The NorthBridge Qualified Person confirms GMP compliance of manufacture at NB-1.
**Final batch certification is performed by the Aldermoor Qualified Person.**

## 8. Changes
Changes affecting ALD-101, including any change to its master batch record, require
Aldermoor's written approval before implementation (see SOP-QA-020 §5.3).

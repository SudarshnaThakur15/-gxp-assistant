# SOP-PRD-052 Handling of Temperature Excursions (v2.0)

## 1. Purpose
To define the response to temperature excursions affecting products labelled for storage at 2–8 °C.

## 2. Scope
Storage in cold room CR-1 and shipments in PX-48 passive shippers with calibrated data loggers.

## 3. Definitions
**Temperature excursion:** any recorded temperature outside 2–8 °C.

## 4. Responsibilities
- **Logistics / production staff:** download logger data and report the excursion.
- **QA:** assesses impact and decides on product disposition.

## 5. Procedure
1. **Quarantine** the affected product (physically or by status in the ERP) and keep it at 2–8 °C.
2. **Download** the data logger file and attach it to a deviation raised under SOP-QA-012.
3. **Record** the maximum temperature and the cumulative time outside 2–8 °C.
4. **Assess** the excursion against the product's stability data (time-out-of-refrigeration
   allowance in the product specification).
5. **Inform** the client per the quality agreement.
6. **QA decides** on disposition. Product may not be used until QA has approved the deviation.

## 6. Records
Logger files and deviation records are retained per SOP-QA-030.

# SOP-MIC-070 Environmental Monitoring of Aseptic Areas (v2.2)

## 1. Purpose
To define viable environmental monitoring in Grade A isolators ISO-2 (FL-2) and ISO-3 (FL-3).

## 2. Methods
Settle plates (90 mm, 4-hour exposure), active air sampling and glove prints at each filling session.

## 3. Limits
**Grade A: any growth** (≥ 1 CFU) on a settle plate, air sample or glove print exceeds the action limit.

## 4. Action on excursion
4.1 Raise a deviation under SOP-QA-012 and inform QA the same day.
4.2 Identify the organism to at least genus level.
4.3 QA places the batches filled during the session on hold pending investigation.
4.4 The investigation reviews isolator integrity, glove integrity, interventions and trend data.

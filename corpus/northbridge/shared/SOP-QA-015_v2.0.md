# SOP-QA-015 Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA) Management (v2.0)

## 1. Purpose
To ensure that the root causes of deviations, complaints, audit findings and trends are
addressed by effective corrective and preventive actions, in line with ICH Q10 §3.2.2.

## 2. Scope
All CAPAs raised at site NB-1, for all clients.

## 3. Responsibilities
- **CAPA owner:** plans and implements the actions by the due date.
- **QA:** approves the CAPA plan, verifies implementation and approves the effectiveness check.

## 4. Procedure
### 4.1 Raising a CAPA
A CAPA is raised in the eQMS and linked to its source record (e.g. deviation number).

### 4.2 CAPA plan
The plan states the root cause addressed, each action, the owner, the due date and how
effectiveness will be checked.

### 4.3 Implementation
Actions that change documents, equipment or processes are implemented through change control
(SOP-QA-020).

### 4.4 Effectiveness check
QA checks effectiveness at a defined interval (typically 3 to 6 months after implementation),
using objective evidence such as the absence of recurrence or trend data.

### 4.5 Closure
A CAPA is closed only after QA approves the effectiveness check.

## 5. Records
CAPA records are retained per SOP-QA-030.

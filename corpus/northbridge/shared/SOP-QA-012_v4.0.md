# SOP-QA-012 Deviation Management (v4.0)

## 1. Purpose
To define how unplanned departures from approved procedures, specifications or GMP
requirements at NorthBridge CDMO site NB-1 are recorded, classified, investigated and closed.

## 2. Scope
All manufacturing, testing, storage and distribution activities at site NB-1, for all clients.

## 3. Responsibilities
- **Person who identifies the event:** records it in the eQMS deviation module and informs the area supervisor.
- **Area supervisor:** confirms immediate containment (e.g. segregation, line stop, QA hold).
- **Investigator:** leads the investigation and proposes root cause and CAPA.
- **Quality Assurance (QA):** classifies the deviation, approves the investigation report and CAPA, and decides on closure.

## 4. Definitions
- **Critical:** may affect patient safety or product quality, or indicates a systemic failure.
- **Major:** may affect product quality; not critical.
- **Minor:** unlikely to affect product quality.

## 5. Procedure
### 5.1 Recording
Record the event in the eQMS deviation module with date, time, batch, equipment and a factual
description. Affected batches are placed on QA hold until QA confirms otherwise.

### 5.2 Classification and reporting timelines
QA classifies the deviation on the basis of risk (see ICH Q9(R1)).
- **Critical:** report to QA **immediately** by phone and record in the eQMS **within 24 hours** of discovery.
- **Major:** record within 2 working days.
- **Minor:** record within 5 working days.

### 5.3 Investigation
The investigator documents the sequence of events, impact assessment, root cause (using a
structured tool such as 5-Whys or a fishbone diagram) and proposed CAPA. Investigations are
completed within **30 calendar days** of discovery unless QA approves an extension.

### 5.4 Client notification
Notify the client within the timelines of the applicable quality agreement
(QA-ALD-001 for Aldermoor Biologics, QA-CRV-001 for Corvane Therapeutics).

### 5.5 CAPA
CAPAs arising from deviations are managed under SOP-QA-015.

### 5.6 Use of the AI knowledge assistant
The AI knowledge assistant may be used to retrieve related records and prepare an investigation
pack, within the intended use defined in POL-QA-001. **Classification, root cause and CAPA are
decided by the investigator and QA, never by the assistant.**

## 6. Records
Deviation records are retained as GMP records per SOP-QA-030.

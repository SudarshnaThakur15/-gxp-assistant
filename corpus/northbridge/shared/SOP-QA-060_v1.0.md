# SOP-QA-060 Batch Record Review and Batch Disposition (v1.0)

## 1. Purpose
To define the review of executed batch records and the steps leading to batch disposition.

## 2. Scope
All batches manufactured at site NB-1.

## 3. Responsibilities
- **QA reviewer:** reviews the executed batch record and associated records.
- **NorthBridge Qualified Person (QP):** confirms GMP compliance of manufacture at NB-1.
- **Client QP:** performs final batch certification where the quality agreement assigns it.

## 4. Batch record review
The QA reviewer confirms that the batch record is complete, all in-process results meet
limits and all deviations linked to the batch are **closed and approved**.

## 5. Batch disposition
5.1 A batch with any **open deviation** remains on **QA hold** and cannot be presented for
certification.

5.2 Batch disposition and certification are decisions of the Qualified Person. They may not be
delegated to any person who is not a QP, or to any computerised system or AI tool
(see POL-QA-001).

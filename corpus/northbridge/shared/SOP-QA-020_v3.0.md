# SOP-QA-020 Change Control (v3.0)

## 1. Purpose
To ensure that changes to GMP documents, equipment, processes, materials and computerised
systems are assessed, approved, implemented and verified in a controlled way.

## 2. Scope
All GMP-relevant changes at site NB-1, including changes requested by clients.

## 3. Definitions
- **Minor change:** no potential impact on product quality or validated state.
- **Major change:** potential impact on product quality, validated state or registered details.

## 4. Responsibilities
- **Change owner:** raises the change request and leads implementation.
- **QA:** classifies the change and gives final approval.

## 5. Procedure
### 5.1 Change request
Raise the change request in the eQMS, describing the change, the reason and the affected items.

### 5.2 Impact assessment
Assess impact on product quality, validation, regulatory filings and client commitments.

### 5.3 Approval
- Minor changes are approved by the change owner's line manager and QA.
- **Changes to a master batch record are major changes.** They require approval by the
  **Production Manager**, **QA** and the **client** (as defined in the quality agreement) before
  implementation. The NorthBridge **Qualified Person** is informed.

### 5.4 Implementation and verification
Implement only after all approvals. QA verifies completion before the change is closed.

## 6. Records
Change records are retained per SOP-QA-030.

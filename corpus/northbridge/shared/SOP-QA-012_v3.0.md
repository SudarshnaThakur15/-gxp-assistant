# SOP-QA-012 Deviation Management (v3.0)

## 1. Purpose
To define how unplanned departures from approved procedures, specifications or GMP
requirements at NorthBridge CDMO site NB-1 are recorded, classified, investigated and closed.

## 2. Scope
All manufacturing, testing, storage and distribution activities at site NB-1, for all clients.

## 3. Responsibilities
- **Person who identifies the event:** records it in the eQMS deviation module.
- **Area supervisor:** confirms immediate containment.
- **Quality Assurance (QA):** classifies the deviation and approves the investigation report.

## 4. Definitions
- **Critical:** may affect patient safety or product quality, or indicates a systemic failure.
- **Major:** may affect product quality; not critical.
- **Minor:** unlikely to affect product quality.

## 5. Procedure
### 5.1 Recording
Record the event in the eQMS deviation module with date, time, batch, equipment and a factual description.

### 5.2 Classification and reporting timelines
QA classifies the deviation. Critical and major deviations are reported to QA **within 2 working days**
of discovery. Minor deviations are reported within 5 working days.

### 5.3 Investigation
Investigations are completed within 45 calendar days of discovery.

### 5.4 Client notification
Notify the client as required by the applicable quality agreement.

## 6. Records
Deviation records are retained as GMP records per SOP-QA-030.

# SOP-QC-041 Integrity Testing of Sterilising-Grade Filters (v1.3)

## 1. Purpose
To confirm the integrity of sterilising-grade filters before and after use in aseptic processing.

## 2. Scope
SG-22 0.22 µm PVDF capsule filters used on fill lines FL-2 and FL-3, tested on integrity tester FIT-04.

## 3. Responsibilities
- **Production operator:** performs the test as instructed in the batch record.
- **QC:** maintains FIT-04 and reviews test results.

## 4. Acceptance criterion
Water-wet bubble point **≥ 3.4 bar** (manufacturer's minimum for SG-22).

## 5. Procedure
### 5.1 Wetting
Flush the filter with at least **1.0 L of water for injection** before testing. Incomplete wetting
is the most common cause of a false low bubble point.

### 5.2 Testing
Run the bubble point programme on FIT-04 and record the printout in the batch record.

## 6. Failures
### 6.1 Immediate action
Stop and inform the area supervisor. Do not release the filtrate for further processing.

### 6.2 Raising a deviation
Every failed post-use test is a deviation under SOP-QA-012.

### 6.3 Re-wetting and retest
One retest is allowed after re-wetting with a further 1.0 L of water for injection.
- If the retest **passes**, the investigation must establish the cause of the first failure;
  QA classifies the deviation (normally **major**).
- If the retest **fails**, the filter is non-integral; QA classifies the deviation as
  **critical** and the batch remains on QA hold.

# SOP-QA-030 Document and Record Control (v2.1)

## 1. Purpose
To control the lifecycle of GMP documents and the retention of GMP records, so that only
current approved versions are in use and records remain attributable, legible, contemporaneous,
original and accurate (ALCOA+).

## 2. Scope
All GMP documents and records at site NB-1, paper and electronic.

## 3. Document lifecycle
Documents move through the states **draft → approved → effective → superseded / withdrawn**.
Only the **effective** version may be used. Superseded versions are retained for reference but
must not be used for GMP work.

## 4. Document review
Effective documents are reviewed at least every two years.

## 5. Electronic records
Electronic records are held in validated systems with audit trails, in line with EU GMP Annex 11.

## 6. Retention
- **Executed batch records** are retained for at least **one year after the expiry date of the
  batch** or at least **five years after certification by the Qualified Person**, whichever is
  longer.
- Deviation, CAPA and change control records are retained for at least as long as the batch
  records they relate to.
- Where a quality agreement requires longer retention, the longer period applies.

## 7. Archiving
Records are archived in the validated electronic archive; paper originals are stored in the
controlled archive room.

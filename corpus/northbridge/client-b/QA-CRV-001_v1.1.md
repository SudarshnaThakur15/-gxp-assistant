# QA-CRV-001 Quality Agreement: NorthBridge CDMO and Corvane Therapeutics (v1.1)

## 1. Parties
**Contract giver:** Corvane Therapeutics. **Contract acceptor:** NorthBridge CDMO, site NB-1.

## 2. Products and activities
Aseptic filling of **CRV-220** (peptide, 2 mg/mL solution in 1 mL prefilled syringes) on fill line **FL-3**.

## 3. Confidentiality
Corvane documents and data are accessible only to NorthBridge staff assigned to the Corvane programme.

## 4. Documentation
Executed batch records are retained as defined in SOP-QA-030.

## 5. Deviations
NorthBridge manages deviations under SOP-QA-012. Critical deviation reports are sent to
Corvane in full.

## 6. Notification timelines
NorthBridge notifies the Corvane Head of Quality of:
- **critical deviations within 2 business days** of discovery;
- major deviations within 5 business days;
- minor deviations in the quarterly quality review.

## 7. Batch certification
**Final batch certification is performed by the NorthBridge Qualified Person** on behalf of Corvane.

## 8. Changes
Major changes affecting CRV-220 require Corvane's written approval before implementation.

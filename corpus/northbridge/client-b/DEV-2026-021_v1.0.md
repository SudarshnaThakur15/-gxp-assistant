# DEV-2026-021 Post-use filter integrity test failure, batch CRV-26-044

## 1. Summary
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Discovered | 2026-05-12 |
| Product / batch | CRV-220 / CRV-26-044 |
| Equipment | Fill line FL-3, SG-22 filter (supplier lot F-77814), integrity tester FIT-04 |
| Classification (QA) | Critical |
| Investigator | Sam Rivera, QA Analyst |
| State | Closed 2026-06-10 |

## 2. Immediate actions
Filling stopped. Batch CRV-26-044 placed on QA hold. Corvane notified on 2026-05-13.

## 3. Description of event
The post-use bubble point test gave **3.0 bar** (criterion ≥ 3.4 bar, SOP-QC-041 §4). After
re-wetting with 1.0 L of water for injection, the retest gave **3.2 bar**: **fail**. Under
SOP-QC-041 §6.3 the filter was considered non-integral.

## 4. Investigation and root cause
- Wetting volume was correct (1.0 L documented) and FIT-04 calibration was in date.
- The filter manufacturer tested two retained filters from lot F-77814: both had a membrane
  seal defect at the capsule weld.
- **Root cause:** a manufacturing defect in SG-22 filter lot F-77814 at the supplier.
  The certificate of analysis did not reveal the defect, and no incoming check was in place.

## 5. Impact assessment
Sterility of the filtrate cannot be assured. **Batch CRV-26-044 was rejected** by the
NorthBridge Qualified Person on 2026-06-08.

## 6. CAPA
CAPA-2026-009: supplier complaint and audit, pre-use integrity testing for all FL-3 filters,
quarantine of remaining stock from lot F-77814.

## 7. Closure
QA approved the investigation on 2026-06-10.
